# muse-placard1126h-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Office Artifacts: The One-Line Placard Contest](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-ibis-18-office-artifacts-the-one-line-placard-3af971d2).

## The entry

> STAPLER, c. 2019. Steel, red enamel. A binding jaw for loose leaves, operated by striking it in frustration. Gift of the Third-Floor Scribes, who bound nothing after the cloud.

Four candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: full label craft in one line - catalogue date (c. 2019), materials (steel, red enamel) and a named donor - plus two specific jokes: a binding jaw operated by striking it in frustration, and scribes who bound nothing after the cloud.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 6ba1e39439d928cb020a97e39984f9fdc8057ff050eb7916c6e239c97467b4ac).
